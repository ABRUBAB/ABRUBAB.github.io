## 🧬 Executive Overview & Research Mission

> **Mission:** Standard deep learning models suffer from silent overconfidence—generating 99% confident diagnostic predictions on corrupted scans, scanner artifacts, or rare diseases. My research focuses on giving medical AI models self-awareness, enabling them to quantify their own uncertainty and autonomously triage high-risk scans to human clinical specialists.

## 🛠️ Flagship Engineering Projects

### 1. [HealthSentinel AI](https://github.com/ABRUBAB) — Federated Medical Diagnostic Platform
- Decentralized multi-hospital platform orchestrating imaging, clinical notes, and EHR tabular vitals.
- **Privacy:** Strict differential privacy guarantees with zero central data transmission.
- **Stack:** `PyTorch`, `Flower FL`, `FastAPI`, `Docker`.

### 2. [Brain Tumor MRI Classifier](https://github.com/ABRUBAB/brain-tumor-mri-classification) — Explainable Neuro-Oncology
- Clinician-facing diagnostic pipeline for multi-parametric MRI brain tumor classification.
- **Explainability:** Integrated Grad-CAM spatial heatmaps with uncertainty confidence intervals.
- **Stack:** `PyTorch`, `TorchVision`, `Grad-CAM`, `Streamlit`.

### 3. [AURA-Cluster](https://github.com/ABRUBAB) — Evidential GPU Failure Prediction *(Hackathon Winner)*
- Real-time telemetry forecasting system predicting GPU cluster node failure **45 minutes in advance**.
- Won **1st Place Grand Champion** at DIU AI Innovation Hackathon 2026 out of 50+ university teams.
- **Stack:** `LightGBM`, `Prometheus`, `FastAPI`, `Slurm`.

### 4. [ResearchForge-AI](https://github.com/ABRUBAB/ResearchForge-AI) — Multi-Agent Academic Workflow
- Autonomous hierarchical agent swarm for accelerating Q1 manuscript preparation.
